## Dati estratti (fonte: abstract, metodo: llm)
- **Disegno dello studio** (`abstract`): Cross-sectional comparison and agreement study
- **Partecipanti** (`abstract`): Competitive cyclists
- **Livello di allenamento** (`abstract`): Trained; competitive
- **Sesso** (`abstract`): Male
- **Età**: _non disponibile_
- **Numerosità campione** (`abstract`): 20
- **Protocollo** (`abstract`): Participants completed a maximal self-paced 20-minute cycling time trial, with average power scaled by 95% to derive FTP, and an incremental exercise test to establish laboratory-based blood lactate parameters.
- **Comparatore** (`abstract`): FTP compared with power at a fixed blood lactate concentration of 4.0 mmol·L−1 (LT4.0), lactate threshold, individual anaerobic threshold, Dmax lactate threshold, and modified-Dmax lactate threshold
- **Durata**: _non disponibile_
- **Outcome** (`abstract`): FTP and laboratory-based blood lactate parameters, including correlation, mean differences, and limits of agreement
- **Risultati** (`abstract`): FTP was 266 ± 42 W and was strongly correlated with LT4.0 (268 ± 30 W; r = 0.88, p < 0.001), with no significant difference (p > 0.05). Mean bias was 2.9 ± 24.6 W, but limits of agreement were large (−45 to 51 W). Lactate threshold (236 ± 32 W), individual anaerobic threshold (244 ± 33 W), Dmax threshold (221 ± 25 W), and modified-Dmax threshold (238 ± 32 W) were significantly different from FTP (p < 0.05). The large limits of agreement refuted equivalence between FTP and LT4.0.
