## Abstract
We live in a day and age where longevity and performance are the greatest aspirations of today's society. Endurance athletes are on a quest to minimize their cardiovascular age and boosting their peak performance. Yet, the key to their ambitions lies in their recovery. Current wearables such as Oura Ring, Whoop, UltraHuman, and Garmin (Body Battery) aim at quantifying recovery by means of scores but remain passive—they monitor and occasionally advise, without intervening. With this work, we envision that earables are in the unique situation where they have all the attributes that it takes to move beyond passive tracking to active recovery interventions. By leveraging continuous vital signs and biometrics sensing, acoustic modulation, dynamic noise masking, and even calorie intake detection, earables could help users actively improve sleep, boost their nutrition and fueling, and autonomic balance. We outline this vision, discuss challenges, and call on the community to explore this paradigm shift.
